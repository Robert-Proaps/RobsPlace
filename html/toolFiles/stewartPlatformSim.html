Platform Pose (jog)

7th Axis — Rotary Head

The head is a turntable mounted on the moving platform. Its axis is the platform normal, so W is kinematically decoupled from the six legs — it never changes a leg length.

Coordinate System

Datum (Z0) = fully retracted. All six legs at minimum length, platform parallel to the base. Because no leg can shorten below that, every tilt or lateral move from Z0 is illegal — raise Z first. The demo program does this on its second line.
ItemConvention
OriginCentre of the base joint circle, Z up
Platform pointCentre of the platform joint circle
X Y ZPlatform-centre translation, mm, from the retracted datum
A B CRoll (X), Pitch (Y), Yaw (Z), degrees
Rotation orderIntrinsic Z-Y-X: R = Rz(C)·Ry(B)·Rx(A), about the platform centre
W7th axis: rotary head on the platform, spins about the platform normal, continuous
SolverInverse kinematics only (pose → 6 leg lengths)
InterpolationCartesian space — pose parameters interpolate linearly, leg lengths follow non-linearly

Supported G-Code

CodeMeaning
G0Rapid move to pose. Ignores F; uses the rapid rates from the Machine tab.
G1Feed move at F. Words: X Y Z A B C W F.
G2/G3CW / CCW arc of the platform centre in the XY plane. Centre given by I J (offset from start). Z A B C W interpolate linearly → helical/screw moves. If the endpoint equals the start, a full 360° circle is produced.
G4 P<s>Dwell, seconds (P in ms also accepted if > 1000).
G20/G21Inch / millimetre input. Angles are always degrees.
G28Home: rapid to the fully-retracted datum, all axes zero.
G90/G91Absolute / incremental. Applies to linear and angular words.
G92Set work offset — redefines the current pose as the given values.
M0Program pause (press Play to resume).
M2/M30End of program.
FFeed, mm/min (modal). Rotary-only moves use F °/min scaled by the 1°≡n mm factor.
; ( )Comments — to end of line, or bracketed.
Feed blending: a block's duration is max(d_lin, d_rot × k) / F where d_rot is the largest angular delta of A/B/C/W and k is the "1° ≡ n mm" factor. This is the common vendor approach — there is no ratified hexapod G-code standard, so this dialect is documented rather than assumed.

Validation

Compiling samples 24 points along every block and rejects the program if any sample exceeds a leg stroke limit, a ball-joint deflection limit, or the leg-to-leg clearance. Offending block numbers are listed in the console and highlighted red.
